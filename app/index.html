One video. A few drawings.

Each frame is one unique drawing to trace. The website will repeat your drawings and restore the dialogue timing when you bring them back.

6 fps · one frame per drawing · silent. This short video carries your drawing library; it is not the finished animation.

  1. Prepare the video, then share it and choose Save Video, or download it to Files.
  2. In Procreate’s Gallery, tap Photo or Import and select this MP4. Each frame becomes a reference layer. Before drawing, check that all reference frames imported; iPad layer limits can cut a video short.
  3. Trace on a new layer above each reference. Group each drawing with its reference so it remains one animation frame. Duplicate your base drawing to reuse the body and background.
  4. Hide the reference layers. Keep one visible drawing group per frame, in the original order. Do not add holds, extra frames, or a ping-pong loop.
  5. Export Actions → Share → Animated MP4 → Max Resolution at 6 fps. Return to this project and choose that video in Assemble.

Keep this project or save a project backup before leaving. Its saved frame order connects your drawings to the dialogue. The app checks counts and size; check the numbered previews for correct content and order.
