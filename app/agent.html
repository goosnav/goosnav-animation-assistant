A short review loop
  1. Validate the recipe and choose a small set of meaningful shot/time samples.
  2. Render and view the PNGs. Check heads, eyelines, limbs, props and overlap.
  3. Change exact JSON paths or fit a camera, then render the same samples again.
  4. Download the recipe or open it in the editor when the images support your choice.
Visibility diagnostics measure rendered pixels and projected geometry. They cannot establish artistic quality. No AI account, remote model or shell is used.
