A short review loop
  1. Plan the coverage. For dialogue: wide two-shot, A close-up, B close-up, return wide.
  2. Validate and resolve nearly identical adjacent shots. Render every cut and meaningful action.
  3. Render and view the PNGs. Check shot contrast, faces, eyelines, gestures, props and overlap.
  4. Change exact JSON paths or fit a camera, then render the same samples again.
  5. Download the recipe or open it in the editor when the images support your choice.
Visibility diagnostics measure rendered pixels and projected geometry. They cannot establish artistic quality. No AI account, remote model or shell is used.
